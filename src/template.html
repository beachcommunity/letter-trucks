How it works

Bear deliveries teaches letter sounds with the big letters he knows. A bear asks for a letter, the truck stops at the loading dock, and he taps a box to load it (with keys or a controller he parks under it and presses the button). The first few times he sees a letter, its box glows after the bear asks, so he gets it right and hears it; the glow fades over the next few times. At first the bear says the letter's name and its sound ("I need the letter S! s-s-sun!"). After he picks it right on the first try twice without a glow, the bear only says the sound ("I need s-s-sun!").

Tow truck brings in the little letters. A bear in a big "B" truck needs its little "b" trailer. Color hints show at first and go away as he gets it right.

A wrong pick just wobbles and says what it is. After two misses the right one starts to glow. Letters he misses come back more often. Each game is a few stops, then it ends with a party.

Extras: he picks his truck before each game (each one has its own silly horn), and tapping the sun, clouds, trees, flowers, birds, cones and puddles makes them do something.

What he's learning

Green dots: sounds (bear deliveries). Blue dots: big to little (tow truck). Two dots means he's got it.

Letters to practice

Letters whose name starts with their sound ("B" is "bee"), then ones where the sound is at the end ("F" is "eff"), then the trickiest ones, where the name doesn't help.

Check the voice

Tap a letter to hear how the bear asks for it by sound ("I need s-s-sun!"). If one sounds off, note which letter so it can be redone.

Controls

Controller: stick or d-pad drives, the bottom face button picks (or jumps between stops), the other buttons honk. Honking near a bear makes it ask again. Keyboard: ← → drive, Space picks or jumps, H honks. Mouse and touch: tap a box or trailer to pick it. To get back here, press and hold the gear in the corner for a second and a half. The way back to the home screen is in here too, so he can't leave a game by accident.
